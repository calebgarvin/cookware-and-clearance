The oven

Cookware & clearance

The space inside a box solar oven isn't a simple box. The glass slopes toward the sun, the oven tilts with the seasons, and handles and lids stick out. Here's what decides whether your cookware fits in My Solar Oven, and a calculator to try your own.

Measure the outside

Handles and lid knobs are usually the widest and tallest points. Measure across the handles and up to the top of the knob.

The glass slopes

The glass angles toward the sun, so there's more headroom at the back than the front. Tall pots go at the back.

Tilting changes the slope

Tilting the oven back for a low winter sun makes the slope steeper, one step at a time. You gain a little room at the back and lose more at the front.

Check the rack's edges

A rim along the sides limits how wide the base can be, though handles can ride above it. The front and back are open, so cookware can hang past the edge a little.

Shape matters

A pot is tallest at the knob in the middle, where the glass is higher, and lower at the edge of the lid. A baking dish is just as tall at its front edge, where the glass is lowest.
