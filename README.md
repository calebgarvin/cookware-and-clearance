# Cookware & clearance for My Solar Oven

Will your pan, baking dish or Dutch oven fit in [My Solar Oven](https://mysolaroven.com)? Pick a common size or enter your own, and see how much room it has under the glass at the back, middle and front of the rack, at every rear leg setting from level to fully tilted.

The page explains what decides the fit: handles and lid knobs, the rack's side rims and open front and back, the sloping glass, and how far the oven is tilted for the season.

All measurements are from My Solar Oven. Sizes in the list are typical, and brands vary, so measure your own cookware if you can and try it in the cold, empty oven before you cook.

The page runs entirely in your browser. It has no cookies, tracking or outside requests.
